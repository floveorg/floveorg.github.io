03

Risa model — Telegram presence, level 3, claim to L4

The risa authy model maps presence and verification separately: L1 self, L2 email (planned), L3 Telegram presence (linked but not claimed), L4 Telegram verified (claimed), L5 phone, L6 bio. Level 3 is the presence profile: you link Telegram, the handle never renders publicly, and claiming upgrades you to L4.

L3 · presence

How to get a level 3 profile

Link Telegram from the ladder above (or the button here). The identity is stored locally in flove:authy; no @handle is published. The same button mints the one-shot claim so you can go straight to L4.

L4 · verified

What L4 unlocks

The bot confirms your DM, Authy stores the verified level, and Appy unlocks publishing. MyNet is already available at L3. Use Send to Appy to hand the level over.

Send authenticated information

From localhost, send the signed authy summary to the keepers. Local first; social only after L4.

Persistence

The authy state is written to localStorage under flove:authy (works on localhost and file://). Appy-basic reads the same key, so the ladder survives a reload and both pages agree on the level.

A real Browsy vault replaces this store later; the schema stays flove-authy/1. P2P permissions are a later trunk.

Risa claim thread. L3 is presence; L4 is the verified claim. The real bot replaces the simulated deep link.

03b

Risa → Appy — two models

Risa already knows the Telegram account behind a clip. Authy is a different bot, so the two models below decide how that proof reaches Appy. Both grant L3 presence only; L4 publishing still needs the Authy claim.

Model A — one-tap handoff implemented

Risa shows a Link Telegram button. The user presses Start once in @AuthyTgBot; the verifier stores risa_key → presence; Appy polls and unlocks L3/MyNet. Two bots, one tap.

A1 · Flow. The risa key is public; the proof is the private DM to @AuthyTgBot. This grants L3 only.

A2 · Sequence. Appy can stay open and poll; no second confirmation inside Authy.

Model B — signed risa claim + Browsy planned

Risa signs a flove-auth/1 presence claim with its key. Browsy’s web of trust carries it; Appy verifies it and grants L3 without the second bot. L4 stays Authy’s.

B1 · Trust. One signature replaces the second bot for presence; Browsy owns the attestation, Appy only verifies it.

B2 · Sequence. The record stays local; peers pull it; Wizy derives on the peer; Nety receives only an optional Social card.

04

MyNet — enabled by Telegram, simplified from MyFamily

In appy-basic, MyNet is not a social network that opens by default. Authenticating Telegram (L3 presence) enables the MyNet features: people, circles, feed and local sharing. Appy shows two lock cards: MyNet local at L3 (people, circles, local feed, invites) and Social publishing at L4 (public posts, public groups). Those features are a deliberate simplification of MyFamily — one person, a relation, a few traits and one affinity — kept as L in Browsy. L4 (the claim) is only needed when MyNet publishes to the social layer.

Channels

Email, Telegram, phone and biometrics are channels with proofs. A proof is signed by the channel and stored as P; only a boolean/level flag is M. Future OAuth drivers plug into the same interface.

Biometrics is currently a channel in the UI; the refined model treats it as a local unlock factor first (pending A09).

Root and masks

One Ed25519 root per human. Masks (nety-frontend) become derived identities or separate keypairs under the same root; they never re-derive the root when a channel is linked (D17).

Multi-mask and multi-device are pending A06.

06

Recovery and rotation

Self-backup (primary)

Seed phrase or offline key. The owner rotates anytime: the old key signs a rotation attestation; the network maps old → new.

No central reset, no probation.

Social quorum (fallback)

A quorum of MyNet/vouchers co-signs a new key bound to the same identity. Standing carries forward.

Short probation window against a coerced recovery.

Compromise

Vouchers/MyNet can flag a compromised key and authorize rotation or revocation, severing the attacker.

Quorum size and appeal are pending A07.
