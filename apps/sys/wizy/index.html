Custom magic insights

Wizy is one quiet custom layer that reads your flove and other portraits you want to add, offering you lovely suggestions and rewards.

One layer, many surfaces. The same portrait is read wherever Wizy is present; the cards you meet depend on where you are in flove.

  • App level — Key fields hint while filling; Exported prompts travel with the portrait.
  • On this index — Custom magic insights frames the nine cards and opens the tour.
  • Inside apps — Appy Magic, Labelers, Raty and Helpers speak at the point of use.
  • Beyond — External embeds and (G)Local Browsy cross the boundary with consent.

04Pending

External embeds

Embedded in external apps and pages, from a micro chip to a full profile.

  • Four surfaces share one portrait: micro, minimal, compact and full.
  • Micro suits footers and mobile; minimal serves inline cards and compact fits sidebars.
  • Full is for onboarding, where the whole state can be shown without a second click.

(G)Local Browsy

Local pulls and local derivation between Browsy peers; a social card reaches Nety only with consent.

  • (G)Local Browsy signs a scoped grant before any peer reads the encrypted projection.
  • MyFamily saves locally and Wizy derives locally: the portrait never leaves the device on its own.
  • No backend and no network: a summary renders the chosen text, not the app’s own rows.
  • The public card publishes only on the owner’s consent; the field test lives in Browsy.
06Live

Appy Magic

Magic button in Appy: Myself joins two of your played apps; With others brings in a MyFamily person and category.

  • Three wizards — Love, Joy and Wisdom — with one active at a time.
  • Myself builds three links, each joining two plays with a different preposition; 3 more appends three.
  • With others writes one sentence from two plays plus the MyFamily friend and their strongest category.
  • The surface is a floating bottom layer over app navigation, opened from Magic → Custom; the app underneath keeps its own controls.
07Live

Wizy Labelers

A labeler re-voices what is already there: headings, buttons and field names speak in a chosen voice.

  • Only the words change; structure, ids and saved answers stay exactly as they were.
  • Eight voices — dramatic, nerdy, cheeky, whimsy, mythic, queer, magical and cool — in one switch.
  • A strip inside the app holds one active labeler at a time, remembered per session.
08Live

Wizy Raty

One rater per scale: a single question asked in one specific way, with 28 scales in the catalogue.

  • Truthy, switchy, hearty and 25 more: the widest list of wizards in the network.
  • Each answer lands in the portrait cell for its own scale, so a rater stays independent.
  • The wizard form beside Raty writes a new scale into the same catalogue.

Wizy Helpers

A help page is a Wizy space too: it explains the interface where the interface lives.

Wisy

Wisy is the wisdom wizy — the Wisdom member of the family, not the whole collection. Wizy is the engine that whispers suggestions; Wisy is its wisdom face, the puzzy-family app that takes one idea and writes it out properly.

Wizy · z

The engine

Reads an app’s summary and returns suggestions. It owns the collection: Love, Joy, Wisdom and the Random wild card.

Wisy · s

The wisdom face

The Wisdom wizard with its own app. It manages the Wisdom labelers — Formal · Casual · Fatal — and turns an idea into a definition.

One idea, five moves

Wisy walks a thought from a single word to a shareable form. Each step adds a layer; nothing is published until you review it.

Essentialname the idea → Perspectiveprism · tone · teleo · field · time → Explainersunfold the parts → Formalisetighten the argument → Summaryreview · download

What Wisy owns

The Wisdom family inside Wizy’s labeler collection: Formal, Casual and Fatal. Its perspective step also carries the Wisdom-adjacent choices — tone (normal · funny · formal · trivial), teleology (experimental · random · fine · fatal), field and time span.

What it gives back

A reviewed definition you can read as one woven phrase, download as a standalone wisy-form.html, or export as wisy-portrait.json. The argument stays yours; Wisy only helps you formalise it.
