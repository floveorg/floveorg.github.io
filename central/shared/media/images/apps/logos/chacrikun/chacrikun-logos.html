How to read. The gama proves the mark holds from a 16px favicon to an 80px cell, plus one living cell on the right. The three animation cells are distinct behaviours, not speeds: each moves a transform or dash-trace on one element and pairs it with an opacity or glow on another — never two transforms stacked. In the diagrams grid, each section unites its animated a/b minidiagram pair (the accent-ringed one is the main pick: 1a · 2b · 3a · 4a · 5b · 6b · 7b · 8b · 9b · 10a) with its formal minidiagram on the right — a plain schematic that redraws the same idea (vectors where a pose matters, bars and lines for the rest) — so every behaviour has both a motion and a static reading. Reduced motion kills every animation and keeps the static mark, exactly what the gama cells show. Night is the default studio; hit ☀️ day to check the pair still reads on light ground — the mark itself never recolours.
