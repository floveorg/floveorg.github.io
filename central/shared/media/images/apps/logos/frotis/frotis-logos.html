How to read. The gama proves the mark holds from a 16px favicon to an 80px cell, plus one living cell on the right. The three animation cells are distinct behaviours, not speeds: each moves a transform or dash-trace on one element and pairs it with an opacity or glow on another — never two transforms stacked. Reduced motion kills every animation and keeps the static mark, exactly what the gama cells show. Night is the default studio; hit ☀️ day to check the pair still reads on light ground — the mark itself never recolours.
