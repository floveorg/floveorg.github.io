How to read. The gama proves the mark holds from a 16px favicon to an 80px cell, plus one living cell on the right. The three animation cells are distinct behaviours, not speeds: each moves a transform or dash-trace on one element and pairs it with an opacity or glow on another — never two transforms stacked. Then each card shows its three views together: the icon (monoline), the animation (hand-drawn, loads on its main one — A breathes, B runs, C is that section's own) and the minidiagram (schematic, the accent marking the one idea). Reduced motion kills every animation and keeps the static mark, exactly what the gama cells show. Night is the default studio; hit ☀️ day to check the pair still reads on light ground — the mark itself never recolours.
