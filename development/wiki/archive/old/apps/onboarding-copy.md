# 10-step onboarding — apps without an intro

This is the onboarding copy for the flove apps that do **not** yet have an
onboarding / first-run sequence. Each block is written to be pasted into the
app's own step system (a CSS-only `:checked` stepper, a `details` panel, or the
app's existing intro modal). Keep the app's own voice; the steps are short on
purpose.

> Status: draft copy, 2026-11-14. `nety` and `browsy` are included for
> completeness but are currently outside the polish programme. `offer`,
> `minioffer` and `questy` are catalog-only (no standalone HTML), so they get a
> note instead of steps.

## Shared shape

Every 10-step sequence follows the same rhythm, so the family feels consistent:

1. Welcome / what this is.
2. The one idea behind the app.
3. The first field to touch.
4. The main action.
5. A second layer / detail.
6. Review what you built.
7. Save or export.
8. Privacy / where it lives.
9. A power tip.
10. Done / start again.

## Goddy

1. **Welcome.** Goddy maps how Goddess, God and Us see one another — a 3 × 3 mirror of the whole.
2. **The idea.** Each cell is a point of view: who is looking, and at whom.
3. **Start with one cell.** Tap **Goddess → Goddess** and choose how fully that seeing happens.
4. **Use the four levels.** *Not seen · see · see more · see fully* — pick the one that feels true.
5. **Fill the row.** Complete the three Goddess cells before moving on.
6. **Mirror it.** Now do the God row, then the Us row.
7. **Read the matrix.** Look for the cells that stayed *not seen* — those are the interesting ones.
8. **Save.** Use the app's save/export control to keep the whole matrix.
9. **Privacy.** Everything stays on this device unless you choose to share it.
10. **Return.** Come back later and change one cell; the matrix is a snapshot, not a verdict.

## Willy

1. **Welcome.** Willy places will — informal and formal — around a cross.
2. **The idea.** Fate is not one thing; it has moods and positions.
3. **Start informal.** Place **FATAL · FINE · LUCKY · RANDOM** on the informal arms.
4. **Then formal.** Place **CHAOS · ORDER · GRACE · FATE** on the formal arms.
5. **Use the edges.** The top and bottom edges are not decoration — put a will there too.
6. **Compare.** Notice where the informal and formal placements disagree.
7. **Adjust.** Drag or tap a placement to move it.
8. **Read the cross.** The centre is the place where the two languages meet.
9. **Save.** Export the placements before leaving.
10. **Reset.** Clear the board and try a different mood.

## Hacky

1. **Welcome.** Hacky is a small mirror: one who, three dislikes, three likes.
2. **Name it.** Write the person (or yourself) in **Who**.
3. **Start with a dislike.** Put the first thing that genuinely bothers them.
4. **Add two more.** Keep each dislike concrete and specific.
5. **Now the likes.** Write one thing they genuinely enjoy.
6. **Add two more likes.** Try to make them different kinds of like.
7. **Reveal.** Use the reveal control to see the full portrait.
8. **Look for the pattern.** A like and a dislike often describe the same value.
9. **Save.** Keep the portrait on this device.
10. **Revisit.** Add one new line after a real conversation.

## PracSys

1. **Welcome.** PracSys lets you practise the eleven biological systems as a living board.
2. **The idea.** Each system has a slot, a reason, and a set of verbs.
3. **Place the systems.** Put all eleven into their slots — start with the ones you know.
4. **Write a reason.** For each placement, say why that system belongs there.
5. **Add verbs.** Give each system the actions it performs.
6. **Open the immune layer.** Add the four immune subsystems.
7. **Thread the slots.** Connect each subsystem to the slot it acts through.
8. **Review.** Read the whole board as one body.
9. **Save.** Export the board when it feels complete.
10. **Practise again.** Reset one system and rebuild it from memory.

## Inventary

1. **Welcome.** Inventary is an inventory of what you have, wish, want and need.
2. **Pick a category.** Start with Health, Food or Home.
3. **Mark one thing.** Tap a bubble once for **have**, twice for **wish**, three times for **want**, four for **need**.
4. **Add a note.** Open the item and write what matters about it.
5. **Set a preference.** For a *have*, use the 1–5 bar to say how much you'd share it.
6. **Say what "shareable" means.** Add *maybe*, *unlikely* or your own condition.
7. **Rate its worth.** Use the 1–8 bar for a *have* you might offer.
8. **Try the multi-view.** Use the wand to select several categories at once.
9. **Save.** Export the inventory before switching device.
10. **Keep it alive.** Revisit one category a week; inventories rot when ignored.

## Keys

1. **Welcome.** Keys is the visual alphabet of flove: words, glyphs and shortcuts.
2. **The idea.** A small set of words unlocks a large set of meanings.
3. **Open a deck.** Start with the deck closest to what you are working on.
4. **Rank the words.** Put the three words in the order that feels right.
5. **Say why.** Write one line explaining the order.
6. **Try a free group.** Build your own group of three words.
7. **Choose favourites.** Mark the words you actually use.
8. **Define a combo.** Give two or three words a shared definition.
9. **Save.** Export the keyset.
10. **Return to the alphabet.** Add one new word when the old ones stop fitting.

## Daty

1. **Welcome.** Daty is a dating profile that starts with what you want, not what you look like.
2. **Say who you are.** Choose your gender and the relationship you are looking for.
3. **Describe the style.** Pick the relationship style that fits.
4. **Talk about children.** Say what you want, without guessing what others want.
5. **Write about me.** Three honest lines are better than a long CV.
6. **Add media.** A photo or a small file is enough.
7. **List your projects.** Current, desired and imaginary — all three matter.
8. **Choose visibility.** Decide who can see the profile.
9. **Review.** Read it as a stranger would.
10. **Publish or keep.** Save it locally, or publish when you are ready.

## Varyy

1. **Welcome.** Varyy is a profile made of questions, not adjectives.
2. **Say "I am a…".** Complete the sentence in your own words.
3. **Answer the first question.** Keep it short.
4. **Use the labelers.** Pick the tone that makes the answer sound like you.
5. **Add a second answer.** Try a different tone.
6. **Review the questions.** Skip the ones that do not apply.
7. **Add a placeholder.** Leave a question open if you want to answer it later.
8. **Read the whole profile.** Does it sound like one person?
9. **Save.** Export the profile.
10. **Revisit.** Change one answer after a real conversation.

## Crumbly

1. **Welcome.** Crumbly is a gentle way to declare what should happen when a promise crumbles.
2. **Name the crumble.** Say what "crumbling" means in this agreement.
3. **Choose the boycot.** Decide what stops when the promise breaks.
4. **Say who is notified.** Pick the people who need to know.
5. **Set the "only if".** Add the condition that makes the crumble fair.
6. **Review.** Read it as the other person.
7. **Make it specific.** Replace vague words with things you can observe.
8. **Agree.** Share it before it is needed.
9. **Save.** Keep a copy on this device.
10. **Revisit.** Change the terms when the relationship changes.

## Evily

1. **Welcome.** Evily is a declaration of what you allow — and what you do not.
2. **Start with "send me to hell".** Choose whether you allow it.
3. **Name the person.** Add who the declaration is for.
4. **Set the curse.** Decide whether a curse is allowed.
5. **Choose the duration.** Use the days field for how long it lasts.
6. **Set visibility.** Private, mediator, community or public.
7. **Add compensation.** Say whether repair is possible.
8. **Add warnings.** Ask to be warned in advance.
9. **Review the permissions.** Read the full list before sharing.
10. **Save.** Export the declaration.

## Profily

1. **Welcome.** Profily is a profile of sub-avatars — the different selves you bring to different rooms.
2. **Create a sub-avatar.** Give it a name and two short info lines.
3. **Choose its souls.** Pick the souls that fit this self.
4. **Set trust and history.** Say how trust is built and what the history is.
5. **Add tools.** Give the sub-avatar the tools it needs.
6. **Pick wants.** Choose the wants this self is allowed to pursue.
7. **Pick offers.** Choose what this self offers to others.
8. **Write the message.** Add the line this self wants to say.
9. **Review the gallery.** Make sure the sub-avatars are distinct.
10. **Save.** Export the profile.

## Sensy

1. **Welcome.** Sensy is a playground for the senses — sight, sound, touch, taste and smell.
2. **Pick a preset.** Start with the preset closest to your mood.
3. **Choose the senses.** Turn on the senses you want to work with.
4. **Adjust the cards.** Change the intensity of each sense.
5. **Add an incentive.** Say what would make the experience better.
6. **Use the annex.** Add anything the main cards do not cover.
7. **Read the disclaimer.** Know what this is and is not.
8. **Review.** Look at the whole sensory mix.
9. **Save.** Export the session.
10. **Reset.** Start a new mix.

## Cathy (Moraly)

1. **Welcome.** Cathy maps your moral stance by placing commandments and sins on an axis.
2. **Choose a word set.** Start with the canonical English set, or the Spanish slang set.
3. **Read the words.** Do not place anything yet — just read the twelve commandments and twelve sins.
4. **Place the first commandment.** Drag it to the zone where it belongs.
5. **Place a sin.** Put it on the mirror side of the square.
6. **Work through the set.** Place all twenty-four words.
7. **Look at the diagonal.** The interesting moral positions are rarely on the centre line.
8. **Switch sets.** Try the other language and see what changes.
9. **Save.** Export the board.
10. **Revisit.** Move one word after a real dilemma.

## Puzzy

1. **Welcome.** Puzzy is the launcher for the language apps: Rate, Relate and Redefine.
2. **The idea.** Every word can be rated, related, or redefined.
3. **Start with Rate.** Open Raty and rate one word on the seven scales.
4. **Try Relate.** Open Linky and connect two words.
5. **Try Redefine.** Open Wisy and give one idea a prism, a tone and a field.
6. **Pick a tier.** Use the tier control to reveal more apps when you are ready.
7. **Read a bubble.** Tap an app bubble to see what it does.
8. **Follow a link.** Open one app from each of the three families.
9. **Save your work.** Each child app saves its own session.
10. **Return to the launcher.** The map is the index, not the content.

## Wizy

1. **Welcome.** Wizy is the small integration page for the flove summary API.
2. **Read the promise.** Wizy does one thing: it turns a summary into a suggestion.
3. **Copy the snippet.** Use the drop-in example as your starting point.
4. **Expose the summary.** Call `window.floveSummary()` in your app.
5. **Render the box.** Call `wizy.render()` with a container and the summary.
6. **Choose a suggestion.** Pick the suggestion that fits the moment.
7. **Test locally.** Open the page from your own server, not a browser preview.
8. **Keep it small.** Do not add data the summary does not need.
9. **Save the integration.** Commit the snippet with your app.
10. **Read the API again.** The page stays as the reference.

## Fity

1. **Welcome.** Fity is a small evolutionary-biology demo.
2. **Read the premise.** The app shows how fit traits accumulate.
3. **Start a run.** Use the main control to begin.
4. **Watch the population.** Notice which traits survive.
5. **Change one variable.** Make the environment different.
6. **Compare runs.** Keep the previous result in view.
7. **Look for the trade-off.** Fitness in one niche is often a cost in another.
8. **Reset.** Start a fresh run.
9. **Save.** Note the result you want to keep.
10. **Return.** Try a different starting population.

## Loly

1. **Welcome.** Loly is the phonology/music side of the language field.
2. **Start with a sound.** Pick a sound or a short phrase.
3. **Listen.** Play it before changing anything.
4. **Change one parameter.** Alter pitch, length or timbre — one at a time.
5. **Listen again.** Compare with the first sound.
6. **Build a small sequence.** Put three sounds in order.
7. **Read the sequence.** Say what the order means.
8. **Save.** Export the sequence.
9. **Reset.** Start a new phrase.
10. **Return.** Add one sound from a language you do not speak.

## Raty

1. **Welcome.** Raty rates a word on seven colour scales and five senses.
2. **Name the subject.** Write the word you are rating.
3. **Add an object.** Optionally compare it with a second word.
4. **Set the seven scales.** Move each slider from *intense* to *banal*, *similar* to *different*, and so on.
5. **Add senses.** Tap the sense icons that belong to the rating.
6. **Open more raters.** Use **＋ More raters** for the extra scales.
7. **Create a rater.** Build your own scale with its own nodes.
8. **Make an axis.** Select two raters and combine them into one axis.
9. **Save.** Export the rating.
10. **Return.** Rate the same word after a week and compare.

## Poly

1. **Welcome.** Poly characterises a text across bipolar pairs.
2. **Write or paste the text.** The main field is the whole point.
3. **Open Declare bias.** Reveal the eight polarity scales.
4. **Set one point.** Click once on a scale to mark a single position.
5. **Mark a region.** Click twice to show a range.
6. **Add more raters.** Reveal the twelve extra pairs if you need them.
7. **Balance the text.** Press **Balance text** to open the opposite field for each rating.
8. **Answer the opposite.** Write what you left out.
9. **Save.** Export the text and its bias map.
10. **Return.** Re-read the opposite fields before publishing.

## Comby

1. **Welcome.** Comby builds fuzzy combinations from words and pools.
2. **Add keywords.** Type a word and press **Add**.
3. **Add more.** Build a small pool of five to seven words.
4. **Open the senses.** Choose which senses belong to the pool.
5. **Add emotions.** Pick the emotions that colour the combinations.
6. **Add systems.** Pick the biological systems you want in the mix.
7. **See the combos.** Open **All possible combinations**.
8. **Select the good ones.** Tick the combinations worth keeping.
9. **Wise them.** Add an evidence, axiom or ethical frame.
10. **Save.** Export the form.

## Confluenty

1. **Welcome.** Confluenty is a seven-column board where lists meet at a centre.
2. **Pick the sizes.** Choose 1, 3, 5 or 7 items per list.
3. **Create a row.** Press **Create** to make a new list.
4. **Fill the cells.** Write one word per cell.
5. **Read the centre.** The middle column is the confluence.
6. **Create another row.** Put a different list beside it.
7. **Use the arrows.** Move a row up or down.
8. **Duplicate a row.** Use **＋** to copy a row and change one item.
9. **Save.** Download the confluence.
10. **Return.** Add a list from a different field.

## Linky

1. **Welcome.** Linky chains a word to a related word, with a strength for each link.
2. **Write the first word.** Start with the word you want to explore.
3. **Add a related word.** Type the nearest association.
4. **Rate the link.** Choose a strength from 0 to 10.
5. **Add another.** Keep the chain going, up to ten links.
6. **Read the chain.** Look at the strengths, not just the words.
7. **Change a strength.** Move a weak link to a stronger one.
8. **Remove a link.** Delete the association that does not belong.
9. **Save.** Export the chain.
10. **Return.** Start a second chain from the last word.

## Vizy

1. **Welcome.** Vizy reads a whole definition back as one woven phrase.
2. **Name the word.** Start with the concept you want to define.
3. **Open the near pills.** Apps, acts, matches, similar, different and prisms.
4. **Pick one relation.** Start with the one that is easiest to answer.
5. **Add a word.** Put one concrete word under the relation.
6. **Open the wizard.** Use the tree to expand the definition.
7. **Add a rater.** Rate the definition from 1 to 7.
8. **Read the phrase.** Let the app weave the parts together.
9. **Save.** Export the definition.
10. **Return.** Add one relation you avoided.

## Vizy2

1. **Welcome.** Vizy2 is the semantic mind-map: relations radiate from a core object.
2. **Name the core.** Write the main word at the centre.
3. **Pick media categories.** Images, videos, audio, docs, links.
4. **Open a branch.** Choose Complementary, Related, Unrelated or Different.
5. **Add an item.** Give it a title and a category.
6. **Rate it.** Use the 1–5 ratings for each category.
7. **Add a child.** Put a smaller relation under the branch.
8. **Use the stream.** The right column lists everything you added.
9. **Save.** Export the map.
10. **Return.** Add one surprising relation.

## Sety

1. **Welcome.** Sety holds the language, scales and defaults for the whole tool.
2. **Choose the language.** English or Spanish.
3. **Set the interaction.** Relates, rates, explains and which apps appear.
4. **Choose the shape.** List, rainbow, circle, sphere or torus.
5. **Tune the navigation.** Recommendations, favourites, recency and simplexity.
6. **Set the relation types.** Similar, complementary, different or ranks.
7. **Set the preferred raters.** Move the sliders toward the voices you trust.
8. **Choose the perspectives.** Pick the lenses you want in the mix.
9. **Set visibility.** Private, close or public.
10. **Save.** Save the dashboard and return to the app.

## Socy

1. **Welcome.** Socy maps society: categories, standards and the relations between them.
2. **Pick a category.** Start with Economy, Legal, Media, Military or Politics.
3. **Read the standards.** Each category has a small set of core standards.
4. **Add a standard.** Write one concrete practice.
5. **Rate it.** Use the hearts to say how well it is met.
6. **Add a relation.** Connect two categories and say why.
7. **Create a category.** Use the form to add your own.
8. **Reorder the board.** Drag a category to a different slot.
9. **Save.** Export the board.
10. **Return.** Add one standard from a real organisation.

## Shareful

1. **Welcome.** Shareful is a lending offer built from clear conditions.
2. **Name the offer.** Write the thing you are sharing.
3. **Describe it.** Add the detail a borrower needs.
4. **Choose the sector and zone.** Help the right people find it.
5. **Set the worth.** Mark what makes it worth sharing.
6. **Choose the time and place.** Where it is, and when it comes back.
7. **Choose who may borrow.** Close, near or all.
8. **Add the reasons.** Pick the ones that are true.
9. **Review the offer.** Read it as a borrower.
10. **Publish or save.** Share it when it is ready.

## Nety

> Nety is currently outside the polish programme; this draft is included so the
> sequence is ready when it returns.

1. **Welcome.** Nety is the friend-to-friend groupware.
2. **Choose a group.** Start with the people you actually talk to.
3. **Write a post.** Keep it short.
4. **Choose the audience.** Friends, groups or close people.
5. **Add a file.** Only if it helps.
6. **Read the thread.** Reply to one person, not the void.
7. **Use a circle.** Move the conversation to a smaller circle.
8. **Check the privacy.** Know who can see it.
9. **Save.** Keep a local copy.
10. **Return.** Post one useful thing, not ten.

## Freed

1. **Welcome.** Freed is a gifting declaration: what you are letting go, and why.
2. **Name the thing.** Write what you are freeing.
3. **Choose the reasons.** Pick the ones that are true.
4. **Tag the thing.** Regular, abundant, new, eco, local, used, vintage, handmade, rare, fragile, heavy or tiny.
5. **Say where and when.** Add the place and the time span.
6. **Describe it.** Write the detail a receiver needs.
7. **Declare ownership.** Say what ownership means for this thing.
8. **Add the wishes.** Past, present, future, world, nature, humanity.
9. **Review the declaration.** Read it as a gift, not a loss.
10. **Save.** Export the declaration.

## Rewardy

1. **Welcome.** Rewardy is a small economy of providers, roles and rewards.
2. **Read the providers.** Each provider has a role and a rating.
3. **Pick a provider.** Start with the one closest to the task.
4. **Choose a role.** Match the role to what the provider actually does.
5. **Set the rating axes.** Use the three axes to describe the fit.
6. **Add a sub-offer.** Break the offer into a smaller piece.
7. **Choose the reward.** Say what the provider receives.
8. **Write the message.** Add the line that starts the exchange.
9. **Review.** Make sure the reward is fair.
10. **Save.** Export the offer.

## Catalog-only apps

These have no standalone HTML yet, so there is no first-run surface to attach an
onboarding to. When they ship, use the shared shape above:

- **Offer** — start with the mode (gift / lend / exchange), then sector, zone,
  worth, time, people and contribution.
- **Minioffer** — start with the type, description, condition, willingness and
  preference.
- **Questy** — start with the app(s), the influence degree and the first batch
  of questions.
