A house for the theory, a voice for the slides

14 Nov 2026 · Tech · flove theory slides whole docs videos

Every long thought eventually needs two things: a house and a voice. A house, so you can walk from room to room and see how the ideas connect. A voice, so someone can stand at the door and tell you what is inside before you commit to reading the whole thing.

flove has both in the making. The slides are the most precious material we have — years of thinking, drawn by hand, page after page, dense in the way a seed is dense. They are also, by nature, hard to enter: you have to already know where to look. The videos are the introduction — a spoken, visual doorway into that material. The last stretch of work was, in the most literal sense, preparation for those videos: building the house the slides will live in, labelling the shelves, and wiring the first room so the voice has somewhere to speak from.

By the numbers

  • 134 commits since the last post (19 Aug → 14 Nov), across 21 active days.
  • Five fields now have a home of their own in the wiki.
  • 20 intro clips and GIFs re-tagged as slides in the docs, and 16 inline players turned into click-to-play stills on the Metaphysics page.
  • Counted, not guessed: commits, days and media entries.

The fields get a house

The biggest change is structural. The field pages — Metaphysics, Science, Biology, Language, Psychosocial — moved out of the site root and into a proper wiki at flove.org/whole. “Whole” because that is the claim: the fields are not separate faculties with separate corridors. They are rooms in one building, and the wiki is the floor plan.

Each field page now follows the same quiet rhythm: the subject, a description, a field title with its substance as a chip on the left, the paradigms as columns (two per row) each with a video slot and an intro underneath, and then the apps that make the field playable.

Metaphysics goes first

The first room is furnished. The Metaphysics page is now split into four sections — Metaphysics, Tele(axi)ology, Theo(onto)logy, (Meta) MathyLogic — each with a short and a formal row, and a pair of paradigms beside it: Simplex and PanBitheism, Compatibilism and Cathy, Confluentism and Cyclon, Intuitionism and Zygoton. Every subsection has a stable anchor, so a link can land on the exact idea instead of the top of a very long page.

That is what preparation looks like: not a finished cathedral, but a room with the sockets in the right places.

The informal door: music, language, and the moving image

Theory has a second entrance, and it is much louder. Under Language → Music live three apps that turn the ideas into sound and play:

ChacriKun, Frotis and ElectrOrchestra reached a mature point — they stand by themselves now.

The standards shelf kept tightening: the apps catalogue gained a five-step intro tour and a calmer grid (the first of many to come for other parts) and bug-free navigation; Futbolia became a modern website too! And more specially: Chatland showed its first motives; Astry now formats a prompt for a private full-analysis search (a basic model to further replicate for other apps); and marc, the author, finally says hello with a more personal intro mini web.

Come poke around. Tell us which paradigm deserves the next video, or argue with a bipolar pair — we are one determined human and one alarmingly patient robot, and the house is only as good as the people who wander into it.
