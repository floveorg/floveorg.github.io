A house for the theory, a voice for the slides

14 Nov 2026 · Tech · flove theory slides whole docs videos

Every long thought eventually needs two things: a house and a voice. A house, so you can walk from room to room and see how the ideas connect; a voice, so someone can stand at the door and tell you what is inside before you commit to reading all of it.

flove is building both. The slides are the most precious material we have — years of hand-drawn thinking, page after page, dense the way a seed is dense — and also the hardest to enter: you have to know where to look. The videos are the introduction, a spoken and visual doorway into them. The last stretch of work was preparation for exactly that: building the house the slides will live in, labelling the shelves, and wiring the first room so the voice has somewhere to speak from.

By the numbers

  • 134 commits since the last post (19 Aug → 14 Nov), across 21 active days.
  • Five fields now have a home of their own in the wiki.
  • 20 intro clips and GIFs re-tagged as slides in the docs, and 16 inline players on the Metaphysics page turned into click-to-play stills.
  • Counted, not guessed: commits, days and media entries.

The fields get a house

The biggest change is structural: Metaphysics, Science, Biology, Language and Psychosocial moved out of the site root and into a wiki at flove.org/whole. “Whole” because that is the claim: the fields are not separate faculties with separate corridors but rooms in one building, and the wiki is the floor plan.

Each field page follows the same quiet rhythm: subject and description; a field title with its substance as a chip on the left; the paradigms as two-per-row columns, each with a video slot and an intro; then the apps that make the field playable.

Metaphysics goes first

The first room is furnished. The Metaphysics page is split into four sections — Metaphysics, Tele(axi)ology, Theo(onto)logy and (Meta) MathyLogic — each with a short and a formal row and a pair of paradigms beside it:

  • Simplex and PanBitheism
  • Compatibilism and Cathy
  • Confluentism and Cyclon
  • Intuitionism and Zygoton

Every subsection has a stable anchor, so a link lands on the exact idea instead of the top of a very long page. That is what preparation looks like: not a finished cathedral, but a room with the sockets in the right places.

The informal door

Theory has a second entrance, and it is much louder. Under Language → Music live three apps that turn the ideas into sound and play: ChacriKun, Frotis and ElectrOrchestra. They have reached maturity — they stand by themselves now.

The standards shelf

It kept tightening: the apps catalogue gained a five-step intro tour, a calmer grid (the first of many) and bug-free navigation, while Futbolia became a modern website too. Chatland showed its first motives; Astry now formats a prompt for a private full-analysis search — a basic model to replicate for other apps; and marc, the author, finally says hello with a more personal intro mini web.

Come poke around. Tell us which paradigm deserves the next video, or argue with a bipolar pair — we are one determined human and one alarmingly patient robot, and the house is only as good as the people who wander into it.
