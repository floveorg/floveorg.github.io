Every long thought eventually needs two things: a house and a voice. A house, so
you can walk from room to room and see how the ideas connect; a voice, so
someone can stand at the door and tell you what is inside before you commit to
reading all of it.

flove is building both. The **slides** are the most precious material we have —
years of hand-drawn thinking, page after page, dense the way a seed is dense —
and also the hardest to enter: you have to know where to look. The **videos**
are the introduction, a spoken and visual doorway into them. The last stretch of
work was preparation for exactly that: building the house the slides will live
in, labelling the shelves, and wiring the first room so the voice has somewhere
to speak from.

## By the numbers

- **134 commits** since the last post (19 Aug → 14 Nov), across **21 active days**.
- **Five fields** now have a home of their own in the wiki.

## The fields get a house

The biggest change is structural: Metaphysics, Science, Biology, Language and
Psychosocial moved out of the site root and into a wiki at
[flove.org/whole](https://flove.org/whole/). “Whole” because that is the claim:
the fields are not separate faculties with separate corridors but rooms in one
building, and the wiki is the floor plan.

Each field page follows the same quiet rhythm: subject and description; a field
title with its substance as a chip on the left; the paradigms as two-per-row
columns, each with a video slot and an intro; then the apps that make the field
playable.

## Metaphysics goes first

The first room is furnished. The
[Metaphysics page](https://flove.org/whole/metaphysics/) is split into four
sections — Metaphysics, Tele(axi)ology, Theo(onto)logy and (Meta) MathyLogic —
each with a short and a formal row and a pair of paradigms beside it:

- Simplex and PanBitheism
- Compatibilism and Cathy
- Confluentism and Cyclon
- Intuitionism and Zygoton

Every subsection has a stable anchor, so a link lands on the exact idea instead
of the top of a very long page. That is what preparation looks like: not a
finished cathedral, but a room with the sockets in the right places.

## The informal door

Theory has a second entrance, and it is much louder. Under
[Language → Music](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/) live three apps
that turn the ideas into sound and play:
[ChacriKun](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/chacrikun/),
[Frotis](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/frotis/) and
[ElectrOrchestra](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/electrorchestra/).
They have reached maturity — they stand by themselves now.

## The standards shelf

It kept tightening: the [apps catalogue](https://flove.org/apps/) gained a
five-step intro tour, a calmer grid (the first of many) and bug-free navigation,
while [Futbolia](https://flove.org/projects/futbolia/) became a modern website
too. [Chatland](https://flove.org/projects/chatland/) showed its first motives;
Astry now formats a prompt for a private full-analysis search — a basic model to
replicate for other apps; and [marc](https://flove.org/marc/), the author,
finally says hello with a more personal intro mini web.

Come poke around. Tell us which paradigm deserves the next video, or argue with
a bipolar pair — we are one determined human and one alarmingly patient robot,
and the house is only as good as the people who wander into it.
