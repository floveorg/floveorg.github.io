Every long thought eventually needs two things: a house and a voice. A house, so
you can walk from room to room and see how the ideas connect. A voice, so
someone can stand at the door and tell you what is inside before you commit to
reading the whole thing.

flove has both in the making. The **slides** are the most precious material we
have — years of thinking, drawn by hand, page after page, dense in the way a
seed is dense. They are also, by nature, hard to enter: you have to already know
where to look. The **videos** are the introduction — a spoken, visual doorway
into that material. The last stretch of work was, in the most literal sense,
preparation for those videos: building the house the slides will live in,
labelling the shelves, and wiring the first room so the voice has somewhere to
speak from.

## The fields get a house

The biggest change is structural. The field pages — Metaphysics, Science,
Biology, Language, Psychosocial — moved out of the site root and into a proper
wiki at [flove.org/whole](https://flove.org/whole/). “Whole” because that is the
claim: the fields are not separate faculties with separate corridors. They are
rooms in one building, and the wiki is the floor plan.

Each field page now follows the same quiet rhythm: the subject, a description, a
field title with its substance as a chip on the left, the paradigms as columns
(two per row) each with a video slot and an intro underneath, and then the apps
that make the field playable.

## Metaphysics goes first

The first room is furnished. The [Metaphysics page](https://flove.org/whole/metaphysics/)
is now split into four sections — Metaphysics, Tele(axi)ology, Theo(onto)logy,
(Meta) MathyLogic — each with a short and a formal row, and a pair of paradigms
beside it: Simplex
and PanBitheism, Compatibilism and Cathy, Confluentism and Cyclon, Intuitionism
and Zygoton. Every subsection has a stable anchor, so a link can land on the
exact idea instead of the top of a very long page.

That is what preparation looks like: not a finished cathedral, but a room with
the sockets in the right places. 

## The informal door: music, language, and the moving image

Theory has a second entrance, and it is much louder. Under
[Language → Music](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/) live three apps
that turn the ideas into sound and play:

[ChacriKun](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/chacrikun/),
[Frotis](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/frotis/) and
[ElectrOrchestra](https://flove.org/whole/linguistics/music/electrorchestra/)
reached a mature point — they stand by themselves now.

The standards shelf kept tightening: the
[apps catalogue](https://flove.org/apps/) gained a five-step intro tour and
a calmer grid (the first of many to come for other parts) and bug-free
navigation; [Futbolia](https://flove.org/projects/futbolia/) became a modern
website too! And more specially: [Chatland](https://flove.org/projects/chatland/)
showed its first motives; Astry now formats a prompt for a private full-analysis
search (a basic model to further replicate for other apps); and
[marc](https://flove.org/marc/), the author, finally says hello with a more
personal intro mini web.
