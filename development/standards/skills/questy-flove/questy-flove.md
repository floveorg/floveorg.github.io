# Questy FLOVE

**Questy FLOVE** is the flove edition of the questy skill: the same structured
project Q&A — read the project docs, detect the gaps and conflicts, ask the right
questions in typed batches — packaged the flove way.

## What makes it FLOVE

- **One file.** The survey lives in a single self-contained `index.html` — no
  build, no network, offline-first, ready to publish to flove.org as-is.
- **Light & dark.** The dark-studio look of the flove family, honouring
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The gauge.** A Flove level bar (1–5) rides along in the survey so every
  session starts with a self-assessed intensity — how deep this pass should go.
- **Guided, not gated.** Everything is visible from the start; nothing hides
  behind the answers.

## The loop

standards/ → plans/ → questions → pendings → answers → next batch.
