About this page

Questy FLOVE dev is the development home of questy's HTML form: a survey becomes a clickable page, answers are saved in the browser, and the selection rides into the next prompt.

Surveys apply live — pick one and its questions appear immediately, and your last state (surveys, search, staged filters, imported packs) comes back on reload. The colour bars narrow by perspective, and replies / pending narrow by answering state; those three stay staged until you press Go. The search box filters the loaded questions instantly.

Fully-answered packs sink into the archive group at the bottom of the surveys list — pick one there to re-open it. Replies are kept on the question cards. A pick that differs from the loaded default glows red and is marked as changed in the exports; a pick that matches the loaded reply does nothing extra.
