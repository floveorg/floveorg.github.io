<a id="voice-note-policy"></a>
## Voice-note policy

> Owner: feedback. Source: archived `feedback-02` hito.

Consent is recorded before transcription; the retention period and who may
transcribe are stated once. A note can be removed on request without leaving a
copy in the index.

<a id="setprivacy"></a>
## Bot privacy — /setprivacy

> Owner: feedback. Source: archived `feedback-04` hito.

The BotFather `/setprivacy` value and its reason are recorded. Group messages are
limited to what the policy allows; a change is a decision, not a silent setting.

<a id="music-love-links"></a>
## Music and love links

> Owner: feedback. Source: archived `feedback-05` hito.

The policy states what may be linked and who can publish it. A link can be
removed without losing the conversation; preview-before-posting is a decision.

<a id="moderated-feedback"></a>
## Moderated feedback flow

> Owner: feedback. Source: archived `feedback-06` hito.

Telegram, Chatland and the moderation rule share one flow. Every item moves
through the same pending → review → live states; exceptions are recorded in Work.

<a id="liberated-laughter"></a>
## Liberated laughter programme

> Owner: feedback. Source: `risaliberada/plan-v2.md` and archived `feedback-07`.

design track; no new backend is implied.
