LocalHostv0 · local-first serving layer

Your own machine is the origin.

LocalHost (v0) is flove’s first serving layer: the solo distro runs directly from your machine — opened from disk or served at localhost — with no server, no account and no cloud in between. Everything else (GitHub Pages, the P2P layer) is a mirror of what lives here first.

how it runs
01

Download

Grab the solo .zip (flove-solo.zip) and unpack it anywhere.

02

Open or serve

Open index.html from disk (file://) or serve the folder at localhost.

03

Service worker

A precache keeps the apps available — even fully offline.

the chain
YOU — localhost / file:// ← origin v0
└── GitHub Pages — flove.org (v1)
    └── P2P relays — nety (v3)
why local-first

Yours by default

Data lives on your device; the network is opt-in. The local copy is the canonical one, and the live site is only its published mirror.
