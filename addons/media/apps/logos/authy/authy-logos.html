About

Authy is the identity and signing module of the Browsy extension. It turns channels into proofs, proofs into levels, and levels into sessions and signed grants.

Tour · 10 slides

  1. The mark — a scan frame around a tick.

  3. The first unlock — a local profile and an identity root.
  4. Channels — email, Telegram, phone, bio; each adds a proof.

  6. Sessions — per-app, time-limited, opened after unlock.

  8. Recovery — self-backup or a social quorum; keys rotate, standing stays.
