About this page

Metaphysics, in every length

Metaphysics asks the most basic questions about reality: what exists, why anything exists, what it is made of, and how its parts hold together. This page gathers flove’s take on that field in every length and format — from a short clip to the full formal presentation.

  • 01 Short & InformalA brief video introduction, plus the songs and a longer 13-minute take.
  • 02 Full & FormalThe complete presentation, the papers and the spoken introductions.
  • 03 Four fieldsMetaphysics (X), Teleology (WHY), Ontology (WHAT) and MathyLogic (HOW) — each with its own short and full row.
  • 04 ParadigmsTwo per field, in a second row: Simplex & PanBitheism, Compatibilism & Cathy, Confluentism & Cyclon, Intuitionism & Zygoton.
