Local data

The layer beneath every field: what lives on your own device.

Local data is the flove root layer — all fields and apps read first from what is stored locally, before any network mirror (GitHub Pages / nety) is consulted. It is where the LocalHost (v0) serving layer begins: your machine is the origin, the web is its published copy.
